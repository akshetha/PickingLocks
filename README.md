WELCOME TO PICKING LOCKS!!

CHECK OUT OUR GAME AT: https://akshetha.itch.io/picking-locks

You've stumbled into an ancient temple looking for treasure, but accidentally woken up an eldritch Goddess from her thousand-year slumber!!! How did you even manage to do this???

Well...good things she's kind enough to spare your life... under one condition...

Use your number keys to select and swap strands of hair to untangle the Goddess's otherworldly locks before time runs out. Dodge fireballs and ancient temple traps along the way, getting hit costs you precious seconds on the clock!

Controls: Number keys (0-9) to select and swap hair strands.
