## 🏺 So... about that treasure

You've stumbled into an ancient temple looking for treasure, but - OH NO - you've accidentally woken up an **eldritch Goddess** from her thousand-year slumber!!!

How did you even manage to do this??? 

Well... good thing she's kind enough to spare your life... **under one condition.** 

Use your number keys to select and swap strands of hair to untangle the Goddess's otherworldly locks before time runs out. Dodge fireballs 🔥 and ancient temple traps 🪤 along the way. Careful... getting hit costs you precious seconds on the clock!

No pressure. It's just an immortal being's hair...and your life. Good luck! 💅

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `0`–`9` | Select & swap hair strands like your life depends on it (it does) |
